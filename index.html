AquaDaemon

Aqua - Latin for water
Daemon - a program that runs quietly in the background

Software and hardware for controlling, monitoring and integrating real-world pool equipment.

AquaDaemon is a collection of open-source projects built to keep pool automation local, extensible and under your control.
